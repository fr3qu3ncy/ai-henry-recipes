Beef Pho (Vietnamese Noodle Soup)

Why This Recipe

A proper from-scratch pho is a big step up from instant soup: char the onion and ginger, toast whole spices, then let a clear aromatic broth do the work. It's the ultimate cold-weather bowl and the freezer trick for paper-thin beef is a technique you'll steal for everything.

Ingredients

  • 1l low salt beef stock
  • 1 large onion, peeled and cut into quarters
  • large thumb-sized piece of ginger, peeled
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 tsp coriander seeds
  • 1/2 tsp cloves
  • 230g sirloin steak
  • 1 tsp palm sugar (or light muscovado)
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 200g flat rice noodles
  • 2 spring onions, shredded
  • 1 small red (bird's-eye) chilli, finely sliced
  • handful of Thai basil, chopped
  • handful of coriander, chopped
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Instructions

Prep

  • Cut the fat from the sirloin steak, wrap it tightly in cling film and freeze for 15 mins — a semi-frozen steak slices paper-thin, which is crucial for cooking in the hot broth.
  • Meanwhile, char the onion quarters and ginger: put them in a dry frying pan over high heat and brown on all sides for 3-5 mins until properly charred (or char under a hot grill).
  • Add the cinnamon stick, star anise, coriander seeds and cloves to the same hot pan and toast 2-3 mins until fragrant.

Broth

  • Tip the beef stock and 500ml water into a large saucepan. Add the charred onion and ginger, then the toasted spices.
  • Bring to a boil, then turn down to a gentle simmer and cook for 30 mins.
  • Strain the broth through a sieve, discarding the solids.
  • Taste and season with the palm sugar, fish sauce and soy sauce — it should taste slightly salty-sweet, stronger than you'd want a finished soup to be.

Cook

  • Slice the chilled steak as thinly as you can, then lay it flat on a plate in the fridge while you finish.
  • Cook the flat rice noodles according to the packet instructions, then drain and divide between two wide bowls.

Serve

  • Arrange the thin slices of raw beef over the noodles in each bowl.
  • Bring the broth back to a boil and pour it straight over the beef — the heat of the stock cooks the beef to just right.
  • Top each bowl with shredded spring onion, sliced chilli, Thai basil and coriander.
  • Serve immediately with lime wedges to squeeze over.

Pro Tips

  • The 15-minute freezer rest is the whole trick — it's the difference between ragged chunks and paper-thin slices that cook evenly in the broth.
  • Make the broth a day ahead and refrigerate — it develops overnight and the flavour gets noticeably deeper. Just reheat, slice the beef, and assemble in 10 mins.
