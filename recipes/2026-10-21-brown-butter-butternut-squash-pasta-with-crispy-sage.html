Brown Butter Butternut Squash Pasta with Crispy Sage

Why This Recipe

Peak butternut squash season and brown butter is your favourite technique - nutty, caramelised butter with crisp sage and sweet golden squash makes a restaurant-style autumn pasta in 40 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340g) dried penne or rigatoni pasta
  • 1 medium butternut squash (~800g), peeled, seeded, cut into 1cm cubes
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 150g (2/3 cup) unsalted butter, divided
  • 12 fresh sage leaves
  • 50g (1/2 cup) grated Parmesan, plus extra for serving
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Zest of 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

Prep

  • Peel and seed the squash, cut into 1cm cubes.
  • Mince the garlic and grate the Parmesan; zest the lemon half.

Cook

  • Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until al dente, reserving 1/2 cup of the cooking water before draining.
  • In a large skillet over medium heat, melt 2 tbsp of the butter. Add the squash and a pinch of salt, saute 10-12 minutes until golden and tender, stirring occasionally; add the garlic for the last 1-2 minutes. Tip the squash onto a plate.
  • In the same skillet, melt the remaining butter over medium heat, swirling, until it foams and turns golden-brown and nutty, 3-4 minutes. Add the sage leaves and fry until crisp, 30-60 seconds. Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.

Finish

  • Return the squash to the skillet with the brown butter. Add the drained pasta and toss to coat, adding reserved pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce is glossy.
  • Stir in the Parmesan, red pepper flakes and lemon zest. Season with salt and black pepper.

Serve

  • Plate and top with the crisp sage leaves and extra Parmesan.

Pro Tips

  • Do not rush the brown butter - it goes from foaming to golden to burnt fast, so keep swirling and watch for the hazelnut smell. Pull it off the heat the moment it turns amber.
  • The reserved pasta water is what turns this from saucy to silky - the starch binds the brown butter and Parmesan into a glossy coating instead of a separate pool of fat.
