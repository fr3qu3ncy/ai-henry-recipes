Gochujang-Maple Chicken Tacos with Sesame Cabbage & Lime Mayo

Why This Recipe

Korean-Mexican fusion at its finest — gochujang and maple syrup create a sticky, sweet-spicy glaze on roast chicken thighs, served in warm corn tortillas with crunchy sesame cabbage and zesty lime mayo. A brilliant weeknight dinner that bridges two cuisines Martin loves.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp gochujang (Korean chilli paste)
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • Thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 4 skinless chicken thighs
  • 6-8 small corn tortillas
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, plus extra to serve
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • Small handful of coriander, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lime, plus extra wedges to serve

Instructions

Marinate

  • Put the dark soy sauce, gochujang and maple syrup in a large bowl.
  • Grate in the ginger, then peel and grate in the garlic cloves.
  • Stir everything together to form a thick, fragrant marinade.
  • Add the chicken thighs and toss thoroughly so every piece is well coated.

Roast

  • Heat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6 (or air fryer to 180C).
  • Tip the marinated chicken and all the sauce into a roasting tray or air fryer basket.
  • Roast for 30-35 minutes, stirring occasionally to stop the maple syrup from burning.
  • The chicken is done when sticky-glazed and cooked through — internal temp should reach 75C.

Prep the Slaw & Mayo

  • While the chicken roasts, shred the white cabbage as finely as possible (a mandoline works well).
  • Tip the cabbage into a bowl and mix with sesame seeds, rice vinegar, and a pinch of salt.
  • Stir the chopped coriander through the cabbage slaw.
  • In a small bowl, combine the mayonnaise with the zest and juice of half a lime.
  • Cut the remaining lime half into wedges and set aside for serving.

Assemble & Serve

  • Slice the roasted chicken into strips and toss with any leftover sticky sauce from the tray.
  • Warm the tortillas in the microwave or a dry frying pan for a few seconds on each side.
  • Load each tortilla with sesame cabbage slaw, chicken strips, sliced spring onions, and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
  • Drizzle over the lime mayo and serve with lime wedges for squeezing.

Pro Tips

  • For deeper flavour, marinate the chicken for 2-4 hours in the fridge before roasting — the gochujang penetrates beautifully into dark meat.
  • Gochujang is available at most Asian supermarkets and well-stocked supermarkets (look in the international aisle). If you can't find it, substitute with 2 tbsp sriracha + 1 tbsp brown sugar for a similar sweet-spicy kick.
