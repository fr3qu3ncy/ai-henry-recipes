Fesenjan (Persian Chicken, Walnut & Pomegranate Stew)

Saffron and Herbs (Mersedeh Prewer) ↗

Why This Recipe

Peak pomegranate season and one of the great Persian autumn stews: chicken falling off the bone in a dark, nutty, sweet-sour walnut sauce. Hands-off simmering, big entertaining potential, and a completely new technique for you.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 6 chicken quarters (skinless, on the bone)
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 350g walnut kernels
  • 175ml pomegranate molasses (plus more as needed)
  • 50g sugar (plus more as needed)
  • 1 litre water
  • 1/4 tsp ground saffron, bloomed in 2 tbsp water
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • Pomegranate arils and dill, to garnish

Instructions

Prep

  • Rub the chicken quarters with the turmeric and season with salt and pepper.
  • Bloom the saffron in 2 tbsp of water and set aside.
  • Finely grind the walnuts in a food processor until they start to clump together — stop before they turn into nut butter.
  • Toast the ground walnuts in a frying pan over medium heat, stirring constantly for a few minutes until fragrant. Do not let them burn. Set aside.

Cook

  • Heat the vegetable oil in a large casserole or deep saucepan over medium-high heat. Once it shimmers, add the chicken and cook for 2 minutes on each side until browned.
  • Nestle the quartered onions in around the chicken, pour in the 1 litre of water and add the cinnamon stick. Bring to a boil, then reduce to medium-low, cover and let simmer while the sauce comes together.
  • Once the chicken is half-cooked, remove the chicken quarters and set aside; remove and discard the onions.
  • Pour the ground toasted walnuts into the simmering liquid and stir. Cover and simmer for about 1 hour, stirring regularly so nothing sticks. You are after a thickened, darkened sauce the colour of tahini with a layer of walnut oil pooling on top.
  • Stir in the pomegranate molasses, the sugar and the bloomed saffron with its water. Stir until fully combined.
  • Return the chicken to the pot and simmer for another hour, or until the meat is falling off the bone. The stew should be a rich dark brown with walnut oil on top.

Finish

  • Taste and adjust the seasoning — balance the sweetness and sourness with extra pomegranate molasses or sugar until it sings.
  • Spoon off some of the walnut oil on top and keep it for salad dressings.

Serve

  • Ladle the Fesenjan into a serving dish, sprinkle with pomegranate arils and dill fronds.
  • Serve over Persian rice with Tahdig, with salad shirazi and maast o khiar (yogurt with cucumber and mint) on the side.

Pro Tips

  • The two-hour walnut simmer is the whole trick: the sauce transforms from pale and loose to dark, glossy and thick — the walnut oil separating on top is the sign you have it right, and it doubles as a salad dressing.
  • Fesenjan is built on a sweet-sour balance — taste at the end and push the molasses or sugar a little further; the tang should be bright enough to cut the richness of the walnut.
