Japchae - Korean Glass Noodle Stir-Fry

Why This Recipe

A vibrant Korean classic built around chewy sweet potato glass noodles, stir-fried with colorful vegetables and beef in a savory-sweet sesame dressing. A great introduction to dangmyeon noodles if you haven't tried them yet.

Ingredients

  • 600g / 1.2 lb bone-in beef short ribs (or 300g / 10 oz boneless short ribs or tender steak)
  • 2 tsp soy sauce (for marinade)
  • 2 tsp white sugar (for marinade)
  • 2 tsp finely minced garlic (for marinade)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (for marinade)
  • 250g / 8 oz sweet potato noodles (dangmyeon), dried
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce (for dressing)
  • 2 tsp white sugar (for dressing)
  • 1/2 tsp finely minced garlic (for dressing)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt (for dressing)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (for dressing)
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • 1 tsp salt, divided
  • 1 onion, peeled, halved, sliced into wedges
  • 3 green onions, cut into 5cm / 2" lengths (white and green parts separated)
  • 200g / 7 oz fresh shiitake mushrooms, stems removed, sliced
  • 2 carrots, peeled, cut into thin batons
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into thin strips
  • 4 1/2 cups (tightly packed) baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp white sesame seeds

Instructions

Prep

  • Cut beef into strips about 5mm thick, 1cm wide, and 5cm long. If using bone-in short ribs, trim excess fat, cut meat off the bone, then slice into strips.
  • Place beef in a bowl. Add marinade ingredients (2 tsp soy sauce, 2 tsp sugar, 2 tsp garlic, 1/2 tsp black pepper). Mix well and set aside to marinate for 15-20 minutes.
  • In a very large mixing bowl, combine the dressing ingredients: 1/4 cup soy sauce, 2 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp garlic, 1 1/2 tbsp sesame oil, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper.
  • Cook sweet potato noodles per packet directions (boil for about 8 minutes). Drain, briefly rinse under cold tap water, then shake off excess water well. Add noodles to the dressing bowl — do NOT mix yet.

Cook Vegetables

  • Heat 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a large non-stick pan (30cm/12") over high heat.
  • Cook the onion wedges, white parts of green onions, and shiitake mushrooms with 1/2 tsp salt for 2 1/2 to 3 minutes, stirring constantly, until just starting to soften but not golden.
  • Pour the cooked vegetables directly on top of the noodles in the bowl — do not mix yet!
  • Using the same pan still on high heat, add 1 tbsp oil. Cook the carrot batons and red bell pepper strips for 1 1/2 minutes, stirring constantly.
  • Add the spinach, green onion greens, and remaining 1/2 tsp salt. Cook for 1 1/2 minutes until the spinach is wilted.
  • Transfer these vegetables to the noodle bowl — still do not mix!

Cook Beef

  • In the same pan, heat the final 1 tbsp oil on high heat.
  • Add the marinated beef and cook for about 1 1/2 minutes, stirring, until very lightly golden and just cooked through — do not overcook.
  • Add the beef to the noodle bowl.

Toss and Serve

  • Now add most of the sesame seeds (reserve some for topping). Toss everything together vigorously until the noodles are evenly coated in the dressing and all ingredients are well combined.
  • Transfer to a serving bowl and sprinkle with remaining sesame seeds.
  • Serve warm (not piping hot). This dish is best enjoyed at room temperature or slightly warm.

Pro Tips

  • The 'mix at the end' technique is key — adding everything to the bowl before tossing ensures the noodles absorb the dressing evenly without getting soggy from the vegetables.
  • Use day-old or slightly cooled rice alongside japchae for a complete Korean meal. The noodles work best at room temperature, so don't rush to serve immediately after tossing.
