Roasted Duck Breast with Damson Sauce

Why This Recipe

James Martin's BBC Good Food classic duck breast technique — pan-seared, star anise baste, finished under the heat — with a twist: the plum sauce is made with damsons, which are at their absolute peak right now in late September. The sweet-tart fruit cuts beautifully through the rich duck.

Ingredients

  • 2 duck breasts (about 450g each)
  • 1 sprig thyme
  • 1 star anise
  • 25g butter
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 250g damsons, halved and stoned (use plums if you can't find damsons)
  • 50g demerara sugar
  • 50ml red wine
  • 300ml beef stock
  • For the sides: 450g Maris Piper potatoes, 50g butter + 1 knob, 50ml double cream, 50ml milk, 250g spinach leaves
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

Prep

  • Heat the oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4.
  • Score the skin of the duck breasts in a crosshatch pattern with a sharp knife, being careful not to cut into the meat, and season well with salt and pepper.
  • Halve and stone the damsons.
  • Quarter the potatoes and drop into a pan of cold salted water.

Cook the sauce

  • Fry the shallot in the olive oil for 5 mins or until softened but not coloured.
  • Add the damsons and demerara sugar, stirring for a few mins until the sugar has dissolved and the fruit is starting to soften.
  • Pour in the red wine and beef stock, then simmer for about 15 mins, stirring occasionally, until the fruit is soft and the sauce has slightly thickened. Keep warm.
  • Meanwhile, bring the potatoes to the boil and simmer for 20-25 mins until cooked through. Drain well, return to the pan and mash, gradually beating in the butter, cream and milk. Season.

Cook the duck

  • Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat and place the duck breasts in, skin-side down.
  • Fry for 6-7 mins without moving them, letting the fat render until the skin is deep golden and crackling.
  • Turn the breasts over, add the thyme, star anise and butter, and baste constantly with the melting butter and juices for 1-2 mins.
  • Transfer the duck (and pan juices) to a small roasting tin and finish in the oven for 5-6 mins for pink, or 10-12 mins for well done.
  • When done, baste once more with the pan juices, then rest on a plate for 5 mins while you finish everything else.

Serve

  • Wilt the spinach with a knob of butter and some seasoning in a big pan for 1 min, stirring, until just collapsed.
  • Thinly slice each duck breast across the grain.
  • Plate with a spoon of creamed potatoes, the wilted spinach and a good spoon of the warm damson sauce. Scatter over the thyme leaves.

Pro Tips

  • Start the duck skin-side down and resist moving it — letting the fat render slowly is what gets you crackling. If the skin tears, you moved it too early.
  • The damson sauce makes 1-2 days ahead and keeps at room temperature (it's a proper cooked fruit sauce), so you can prep it before and keep everything on the day calm. If you can't get damsons, dark red plums work almost identically.
