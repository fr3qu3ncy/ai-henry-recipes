Crispy Gnocchi with Cherry Tomato Sauce

Why This Recipe

Crispy gnocchi is a game-changing technique — searing store-bought potato dumplings until golden and crackly, then tossing them with a jammy cherry tomato sauce. Peak-summer tomatoes, 30 minutes, one skillet, and a whole new way to use the humble gnocchi package.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, divided
  • 20 ounces shelf-stable or refrigerated potato gnocchi (2 x 10-ounce packages)
  • 1 pint (10 ounces) cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup roasted red peppers, chopped
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • Zest from 1 lemon
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced basil leaves, plus more for serving
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more for serving

Instructions

Crisp the Gnocchi

  • In a large skillet over medium heat, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil for about 30 seconds until shimmering.
  • Add the first package of gnocchi in a single layer — do not crowd the pan. Cook undisturbed until golden brown on one side, about 2 minutes.
  • Flip and cook until evenly golden brown on all sides, about 2 minutes more. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
  • Repeat with the second package of gnocchi, adding another tablespoon of olive oil to the pan.

Build the Tomato Sauce

  • Add the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil to the same skillet and heat until shimmering.
  • Add the halved cherry tomatoes, chopped roasted red peppers, thinly sliced red onion, grated garlic, lemon zest, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes (if using).
  • Cook for 7 to 9 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes begin to burst and release their juices and the onions have softened. The sauce should be jammy and glossy.

Combine and Serve

  • Reduce the heat to low. Return the crispy gnocchi to the skillet along with the basil and Parmigiano-Reggiano.
  • Toss gently to combine — the cheese will melt slightly into the warm sauce, creating a light coating on each gnocchi.
  • Taste and season with more salt and pepper if needed.
  • Serve immediately, topped with extra basil and Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Pro Tips

  • Do not skip the 'cook undisturbed' step — letting the gnocchi sit without moving it is what creates the crispy golden crust. Stirring too early steams them instead of searing them.
  • Use shelf-stable gnocchi (the firm, slightly dry kind from the pasta aisle) rather than fresh gnocchi — they crisp up far better. Fresh gnocchi are softer and more fragile, making them harder to get crispy.
  • The roasted red peppers add a smoky depth that elevates the sauce beyond simple tomatoes. Use high-quality jarred peppers or roast your own for maximum flavor.
