One Pan Jambalaya

Why This Recipe

A bold Cajun one-pot wonder — smoky andouille, spicy chicken, and rice all simmered together in a single skillet. Perfect for when you want maximum flavor with minimum cleanup.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp vegetable or canola oil
  • 1 lb andouille sausage, cut into ¼ inch thick slices
  • 1½ lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 yellow or white onion, chopped
  • 4 green onions, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 3 ribs celery, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1½ tsp dried basil
  • 1½ tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (add more for extra heat)
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1¼ cups long-grain white rice

Instructions

Brown the Meat

  • In a large skillet with a fitted lid, heat oil over medium-high heat.
  • Add chicken pieces and cook, flipping once or twice, until browned on all sides (don't cook through). Remove to a bowl.
  • Add sausage slices to the same pan and cook until browned on both sides. Add to the bowl with the chicken.

Build the Base

  • Reduce heat to medium. Add butter and flour to the pan, stirring well and scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
  • Add onion, garlic, celery, and bell peppers. Sauté for 3 minutes until softened.

Simmer

  • Add dried basil, Cajun seasoning, diced tomatoes (with juices), salt, and pepper. Stir well to combine.
  • Pour in chicken broth and add rice. Bring to a gentle boil.
  • Return the chicken and sausage to the pan. Reduce heat to low, cover tightly with the lid, and cook for 20 minutes.

Rest and Serve

  • Remove from heat. Let rest, still covered, for 10 minutes — this is crucial for the rice to finish absorbing liquid.
  • Gently fluff with a fork. Rest for another 5 minutes before serving.

Pro Tips

  • Don't skip the resting step — the 15 minutes of resting time is what allows the rice to finish cooking evenly without burning.
  • For a deeper flavor, use chicken thighs instead of breasts and add a splash of hot sauce to the broth.
