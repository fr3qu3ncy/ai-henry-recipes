Dan Dan Noodles

Why This Recipe

Dan Dan Noodles are one of 2026's biggest trending noodle dishes — a punchy Sichuan bowl that's basically the ultimate weeknight meal: 30 minutes, one wok, and that addictive numbing chilli-sesame sauce that takeout never quite nails.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp Chinese sesame paste (sub tahini)
  • 1.5 tbsp Chinese chilli paste in oil (adjust to taste)
  • 4 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tsp white sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Chinese five spice powder
  • 1 tsp Sichuan pepper powder, preferably freshly ground
  • 3 tbsp (or more!) chilli oil, preferably Chinese
  • 3/4 cup (185ml) chicken stock, hot, low sodium
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil (for pork)
  • 250g (8oz) pork mince
  • 2 tsp Hoisin sauce
  • 1 tsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine (sub 2 tbsp extra stock)
  • 1/2 tsp Chinese five spice powder (for pork)
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil (for greens)
  • 30g (1/4 cup) Sui Mi Ya Cai (preserved mustard greens), finely chopped
  • 500g (1lb) white fresh noodles, medium thickness
  • 16 choy sum stems, cut into 15cm pieces
  • 2 green onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tbsp peanuts, finely chopped (optional)

Instructions

Prep

  • Mix the dan dan sauce: whisk together sesame paste, chilli paste, light soy sauce, minced garlic, sugar, 1/2 tsp five spice, Sichuan pepper powder and chilli oil. Gently stir in the hot chicken stock last — the oil should sit on the surface. Set aside.
  • Mix the pork marinade: hoisin, 1 tsp dark soy, cooking wine and 1/2 tsp five spice.
  • Finely chop the Sui Mi Ya Cai and green onions. Finely chop the peanuts.

Cook

  • Heat the vegetable oil in a skillet or wok over high heat. Add the pork and cook, breaking it up, until it changes from pink to white.
  • Add the pork marinade and cook for 1 minute, then tip the pork into a bowl.
  • Return the skillet to medium heat. Add a teaspoon of oil to the middle of the pan, add the Sui Mi Ya Cai and stir for 30 seconds, just to warm through. Set aside.
  • Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the noodles per packet directions (about 2-3 min for fresh). Add the choy sum for the last 1 minute of cooking, then drain well.

Serve

  • Ladle 1/4 of the dan dan sauce into each of 4 bowls. Pile in the noodles, top with the crispy pork and warmed Sui Mi Ya Cai, and place the choy sum on the side.
  • Sprinkle over the peanuts and green onions. Mix it all up to coat the noodles well with sauce, then devour immediately.

Pro Tips

  • The chilli oil is a key part of dan dan noodles — it's not just flavour, it's mouthfeel and the glossy look. Go for a proper Chinese chilli oil if you can, and don't skimp.
  • Ground Sichuan peppercorns give that signature mǎlā numbing buzz. Grind them fresh in a mortar for a punchier finish, and keep the spice at a moderate background level — the fragrance should lead, not the burn.
