Roast Fallow Rump with Marmite & Chive Butter

Why This Recipe

Fallow venison season is kicking off and this from José Souto is a brilliant 4-ingredient game dinner — the rump renders fat-side down to a crispy crust, then warm slices of savoury Marmite chive butter melt over the meat. Peak late-autumn game cookery, easy to nail.

Ingredients

  • 100g butter, at room temperature
  • 10g Marmite
  • 2 tsp finely chopped chives
  • 1 D-shaped venison (fallow) rump, fat side on

Instructions

Prep

  • Softened butter goes in a bowl with the Marmite and chopped chives; mix until evenly combined.
  • Lay the seasoned butter on greaseproof paper, roll the paper into a tight cylinder, and refrigerate to set (at least 30 min — so make this first).
  • Trim the rump, leaving a 1cm layer of outer fat, and score the fat in a criss-cross pattern.

Cook

  • Heat a heavy pan over medium-high heat and season the meat well.
  • Place the rump fat-side down and cook until the fat has rendered to a crispy ~5mm covering, basting with the rendered fat as it cooks.
  • Turn and seal the other side, basting frequently, until cooked to at most medium (venison dries out quickly past that).
  • Transfer to a warm plate and rest for a few minutes.

Serve

  • Cut the chilled butter log into 5mm slices through the paper.
  • Slice the rested rump and lay a slice of Marmite chive butter on each while still warm, so it melts.
  • Serve with plenty of seasonal produce — roast roots, greens, or a proper game-style accompaniment.

Pro Tips

  • Make the butter first — it needs to fully set in the fridge before it can be sliced cleanly, so it's the make-ahead element.
  • Keep it at medium at most: venison rump is lean and overcooks fast. The crispy rendered fat layer is what keeps the slices juicy and adds the texture contrast.
