Chorizo, Cannellini Bean & Butternut Squash Stew

Why This Recipe

Butternut squash and chestnuts have just hit peak season, and this one-pot Spanish-style stew turns them into a bold, garlicky, deeply savoury weeknight dinner with barely any effort — 35 minutes, one pan, no faff.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated or crushed
  • 200g chorizo, chopped into 1cm chunks
  • 500g butternut squash (roughly half a large one), chopped into 1cm chunks
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 100ml chicken stock (from a cube is fine)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 400g tin cannellini beans, drained
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley (plus extra for sprinkling)
  • Mashed potatoes and greens, to serve

Instructions

Prep

  • Dice the onion, grate or crush the garlic, and chop the chorizo and butternut squash into 1cm / half-inch chunks — keep them uniform so everything cooks evenly.

Sweat & Fry

  • Heat the olive oil in a wide, deep saucepan, add the onion, garlic and chorizo, cover with a lid and cook on low heat for 3 minutes until the onions are softened but not browned, stirring occasionally.
  • Add the butternut squash, stir and fry gently for a further 2 minutes.

Simmer

  • Add the chopped tomatoes, stock and a good pinch of salt and pepper. Bring to the boil, then turn down and simmer for 10 minutes.
  • Use this time to mash your potatoes and prep your greens.
  • After 10 minutes add the drained cannellini beans and simmer for a further 10 minutes, until the squash is tender when pierced with a fork.
  • Stir through the chopped parsley and taste for seasoning.

Serve

  • Spoon the stew into warmed bowls with the mashed potatoes and greens, scatter with extra parsley, and serve with a Spanish red.

Pro Tips

  • Go for a smoked, garlicky Spanish-style chorizo (not sweet Italian) — the smoke and paprika are what carry the flavour.
  • To deepen it: stir in a splash of sherry or a pinch of smoked paprika with the tomatoes, and finish with a little parmesan instead of parsley for extra umami.
