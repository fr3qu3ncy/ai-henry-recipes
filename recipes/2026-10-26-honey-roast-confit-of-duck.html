Honey-Roast Confit of Duck

Why This Recipe

Confit is the classic French technique of preserving meat in its own fat - duck legs slow-cooked for 2.5 hours until meltingly tender, then crisped in a sticky honey glaze. Hands-off, make-ahead, and a brilliant entertaining dish as winter draws in.

Ingredients

  • 3 thyme sprigs, leaves roughly chopped
  • 2-3 rosemary sprigs, leaves roughly chopped
  • 2 fat garlic cloves, lightly bashed
  • 1/2 tsp black peppercorns, roughly crushed
  • 4 duck leg joints (about 800g)
  • 750g duck fat
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Sunflower oil, for covering the duck if needed
  • 4 tbsp clear honey

Instructions

Prep (the day before)

  • Mix the thyme, rosemary, garlic, peppercorns with 2 tbsp flaky sea salt.
  • Tip half the salt mix into a large baking dish, top with the duck legs, then scatter over the remaining salt and massage it well into the duck.
  • Cover with cling film and chill in the fridge for 24 hours.

Confit

  • Heat oven to 140C/120C fan/gas 1. Take the duck legs out and wipe off the salt mixture with kitchen paper.
  • Melt the duck fat in a flameproof casserole over a low heat until just bubbling.
  • Add the bay leaves and duck legs, and bring to a gentle simmer - the legs must be completely submerged, so top up with a splash of oil if needed.
  • Transfer to the oven and cook for 2 1/2 hours, until a skewer inserted into the thickest part of a leg goes in really easily.
  • Leave the duck to cool in the fat. It can now be chilled in the fat for up to 2 weeks.

Finish & Serve

  • Heat oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4. Lift the duck legs from the fat, scrape off the excess, and place skin-side up in a shallow roasting tin.
  • Spoon 1 tbsp of the honey over each leg and roast for 25-30 minutes until the skin is deeply browned and sticky.
  • Serve with the reserved confit fat warmed as a gravy, a crisp salad, and roast potatoes or Puy lentils.

Pro Tips

  • Save the rendered confit fat in a jar - it keeps in the fridge for months and makes the best roast potatoes or sauteed vegetables around.
  • The salted duck can go straight into the freezer for up to 3 months, and the finished confit lasts weeks under its fat - this dish practically plans your week for you.
