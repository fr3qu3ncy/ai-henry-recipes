Peruvian Anticuchos (Aji Panca Beef Skewers)

Why This Recipe

A thousand-year-old Andean street food rarely seen on UK menus: beef marinated in a fruity, smoky aji panca chilli paste, then charred hard over high heat. The marinade does the heavy lifting, so hands-on time is under 30 minutes for a big-flavour weekend grill.

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • 1/3 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, diced
  • 2 tbsp aji panca powder (Peruvian dried red chilli) — see Pro Tips if you can't source it
  • 1/2 lime, juiced
  • 1.5 lbs (680g) beef sirloin steak, cut into 2cm cubes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil (for marinade)
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Steamed rice or boiled potatoes, to serve

Instructions

Prep

  • Soak wooden skewers in water for 30 minutes to prevent splitting and burning (skip if using metal).
  • Make the aji panca paste: heat 2 tbsp oil in a small saucepan, add the diced onion and sauté for 2-3 minutes until softened.
  • Add the garlic and aji panca powder and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Stir in the remaining 2 tbsp oil and the lime juice, then remove from heat and let the paste cool.

Marinate

  • In a large zip bag or non-reactive bowl, combine the beef cubes with the aji panca paste, 2 tbsp oil, white wine vinegar and salt.
  • Coat the beef well, cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours — or up to overnight for deeper flavour.

Grill

  • Heat a grill or barbecue over medium-high heat until properly hot.
  • Thread the beef cubes onto the skewers, packing them snugly.
  • Grill for about 6 minutes total for medium rare, turning every 2 minutes until charred on the edges and just pink in the middle.

Serve

  • Serve hot over steamed rice or boiled potatoes, with any pan juices spooned over.

Pro Tips

  • Aji panca is the soul of this dish but hard to find in the UK. Best stand-in: 1.5 tbsp sweet smoked paprika + 1 tsp dried red chilli powder (guajillo or ordinary dried chilli) + a pinch of sugar — it reproduces the fruity, slightly sweet, smoky depth that pure chilli powder lacks.
  • No barbecue access? Bake the skewers at 180C (350F) for 15-20 minutes, turning halfway, then broil for 2-3 minutes at the end to char the edges.
