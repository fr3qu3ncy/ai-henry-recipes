Cold Soba Noodle Salad with Strawberries

Why This Recipe

A beautiful late-summer dish from Susan Spungen's 'Open Kitchen' — soba noodles dressed in tangy miso, topped with charred shishitos, crisp cucumber, and fresh strawberries. The sweet-tart berries contrast brilliantly with the salty-spicy dressing. Almost no-cook, ready in 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz soba noodles
  • 1/4 cup rice wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 hot red chile, finely minced (or more to taste)
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 4 oz shishito peppers (or poblano peppers if out of season)
  • 2 Persian cucumbers, peeled, seeded and thinly sliced on the bias
  • 8 oz strawberries, hulled and halved (about 2 cups)
  • 1 small red chile, thinly sliced
  • 2 scallions, white and green parts, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, roughly chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro leaves, roughly chopped

Instructions

Prep

  • Cook soba noodles according to package directions (usually 4-5 minutes in boiling water). Drain and rinse thoroughly under cold water until completely cool. Set aside.
  • In a small bowl, whisk together rice wine vinegar, sugar, and salt until the sugar and salt dissolve completely.
  • Add white miso paste, minced red chile, and toasted sesame oil to the vinegar mixture. Whisk vigorously until the miso is fully incorporated and the dressing is smooth. Set aside.

Cook

  • Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill pan over high heat until very hot. Add shishito peppers (no oil needed — they release their own).
  • Cook shishitos for about 3 minutes per side, turning occasionally, until the skins are deeply blistered and charred in spots and the peppers have softened. Remove from heat and let cool.
  • Once cooled, slice shishitos on a diagonal, using a knife tip to avoid the seeds (some contain surprise heat).

Assemble

  • Place the cooled soba noodles in a large serving bowl. Pour the miso dressing over the noodles and toss gently until evenly coated.
  • Add the grilled shishito peppers, sliced cucumbers, halved strawberries, sliced red chile, scallions, mint, and cilantro to the bowl.
  • Mix everything together gently — fold rather than stir to keep the strawberries intact.

Serve

  • Serve immediately at room temperature, or chill briefly (15-20 min) for a cooler salad.
  • Add extra chile or a drizzle of sesame oil if you want more kick.
  • Tip: Add strawberries only at the last minute so they stay fresh and don't get mushy.

Pro Tips

  • Make-ahead friendly: Cook and rinse the noodles up to a day ahead, store in the fridge with a light coating of oil. The dressing can also be made ahead and refrigerated.
  • The miso dressing tastes thick when first mixed but thins out as it coats the noodles — don't add extra liquid. If you prefer a milder dressing, reduce the chile; for more heat, add gochujang instead of miso.
