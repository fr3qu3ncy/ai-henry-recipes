Wild Mushroom Galettes

Why This Recipe

Mushroom season is peaking right now, and Mary Berry takes her classic garlic-mushroom vol-au-vent and turns it into six free-form galettes. Golden puff cases packed with browned wild mushrooms in a crème fraîche and Parmesan sauce — proper autumnal, and a brilliant showstopper for a light lunch or starter.

Ingredients

  • 500g block ready-made puff pastry
  • plain flour, for dusting
  • 1 free-range egg, beaten
  • 3 tbsp sunflower oil
  • knob of butter
  • 1 large garlic clove, crushed
  • 250g chestnut mushrooms, quartered
  • 250g large flat mushrooms, cut into 8 wedges
  • 250g mixed wild mushrooms (oyster, shiitake), sliced
  • 400g full-fat crème fraîche
  • 50g Parmesan or vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
  • juice of 1/2 lemon
  • finely chopped flat-leaf parsley, to garnish
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

Prep

  • Preheat the oven to 200C/180C fan/Gas 6 and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
  • On a floured work surface, roll the puff pastry into a 28x20cm rectangle about 3mm thick.
  • Lightly score a criss-cross pattern over the surface (do not cut through), then stamp out six rounds with an 8.5cm/3in cutter.
  • Chill the pastry rounds on the tray for 10 minutes, then brush the tops with the beaten egg.

Cook

  • Put a large roasting tin in the oven to get very hot. Add the oil, butter and garlic, then the chestnut and flat mushrooms. Season and toss.
  • Put the tin on the bottom of the oven and the pastry tray near the top. Bake 18-20 minutes — pastry should be well risen and lightly golden; the mushrooms should be brown with most of their juices evaporated.
  • Remove both trays. Slice the galettes in half widthways, peel out any uncooked pastry from the middles, and turn the tops upside down on the baking sheet.
  • Add the wild mushrooms and crème fraîche to the roasting tin and mix. Return both trays to the oven for 8-10 minutes until the pastry cases are crisp and the crème fraîche has turned into a mushroom-coloured sauce.
  • Stir the Parmesan and lemon juice into the mushroom sauce and season well.

Serve

  • Spoon the mushroom filling into half the pastry cases.
  • Garnish with chopped parsley, top with the other pastry halves and serve immediately.

Pro Tips

  • Browning the mushrooms properly is the whole game — if the juices are still there when you plate, the filling will be soggy. A screaming-hot tin and a low oven shelf do the dehydrating work for you.
  • Can t find wild oyster or shiitake? Two different shop-bought varieties (chestnut + king oyster) still give you the layered texture and flavour you want.
