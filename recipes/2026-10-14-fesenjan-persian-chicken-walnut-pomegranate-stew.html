Fesenjan (Persian Chicken, Walnut & Pomegranate Stew)

Why This Recipe

Peak pomegranate season and one of the great Persian stews: chicken braised in a deep, tangy walnut sauce. A slow-simmer that rewards patience with a glossy, sweet-sour sauce that gets even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 6 chicken quarters (skinless, on the bone)
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 350g walnut kernels
  • 175ml pomegranate molasses (plus more as needed)
  • 50g sugar (plus more as needed)
  • 1 litre water
  • 1/4 tsp ground saffron, bloomed in 2 tbsp warm water
  • Salt & black pepper to taste
  • Pomegranate arils & dill fronds to garnish

Instructions

Prep

  • Rub the chicken quarters with the turmeric and season well with salt and pepper.
  • Bloom the saffron in 2 tbsp warm water and set aside.
  • Finely grind the walnuts in a food processor until they start to clump together — stop before they turn into nut butter.
  • Lightly toast the ground walnuts in a frying pan over medium heat for a few minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant. Don't let them burn. Set aside.

Cook

  • Heat the vegetable oil in a large casserole or deep saucepan with a lid over medium-high heat. Once it shimmers, add the chicken and cook for about 2 minutes per side until nicely browned.
  • Nestle the quartered onion in amongst the chicken, add 1 litre of water and the cinnamon stick. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a medium-low simmer, cover, and let the chicken cook through (about 25-30 min) while you keep an eye on the walnuts.
  • Lift out the chicken quarters and set aside; remove and discard the softened onions (or keep them for soup).
  • Pour the toasted ground walnuts into the simmering liquid and stir. Cover and simmer for about 1 hour, stirring regularly so the walnuts don't stick — the sauce should thicken, darken to a deep brown like tahini, and release a layer of walnut oil on the surface.

Finish

  • Stir in the pomegranate molasses, sugar and the bloomed saffron with its water until fully combined.
  • Return the chicken quarters to the stew and simmer for a further hour, until the meat is falling off the bone. The stew should be rich dark brown with a thin sheen of walnut oil on top — spoon some off for salad dressing later.

Serve

  • Taste and adjust the balance of sweet and sour to your liking, adding a splash more molasses or a little sugar as needed.
  • Ladle into a serving dish, scatter with pomegranate arils and dill fronds, and serve with saffron Persian rice (chelow with tahdig), Salad Shirazi and yogurt-cucumber (maast o khiar).

Pro Tips

  • Fesenjan genuinely improves overnight — the walnuts and pomegranate keep marrying in the fridge. Cook it the day before you plan to eat it and reheat gently with a splash of water.
  • Don't skip toasting the ground walnuts — raw walnut sauce tastes flat and pale. Toasting unlocks a deep, almost chocolatey aroma and is what gives the finished stew its dark, glossy colour.
