Kimchi Jjigae (Korean Kimchi & Pork Belly Stew)

Why This Recipe

Spicy, warming Korean comfort food built on properly fermented mature kimchi — the deeper the funk, the better the broth. A perfect winter weeknight pot that simmers itself while you prep the rest of the meal.

Ingredients

  • 400g mature kimchi
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/4 small brown onion, finely sliced
  • 200g pork belly, finely sliced
  • 1 1/2 tsp gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes)
  • 1 tsp caster sugar
  • 150g firm tofu, drained
  • 2 spring onions, finely chopped

Instructions

Prep

  • Drain the kimchi in a sieve over a measuring jug — reserve about 100ml of the brine (top up from the jar if needed); the brine is the flavour base of the broth.
  • Slice the tofu into 5cm squares about 1cm thick and set aside.

Cook

  • Heat the vegetable oil in a large saucepan over high heat and fry the drained kimchi, brown onion and pork belly for 5 mins, until the pork starts to colour.
  • Stir in the gochugaru and sugar, then fry for another 2 mins to toast the chilli and deepen the spice.
  • Pour in 800ml water plus the reserved kimchi brine and bring to a boil.
  • Simmer, uncovered, over high heat for 15 mins, skimming off any foam that rises to keep the broth clean and punchy.

Serve

  • Arrange the tofu squares over the simmering stew, cover with a lid and cook for 3-4 mins until heated through.
  • Scatter over the spring onions and serve straight from the pan with a bowl of steamed rice.

Pro Tips

  • Maturity is everything — use kimchi that has been fermenting for a few weeks (tangy, funky, not just fresh). Young kimchi makes a pale, flat broth.
  • Keep the kimchi brine at all costs; it is salt, lactic acid and depth in one. Top it up with water if you run short, but never swap it out for plain water alone.
