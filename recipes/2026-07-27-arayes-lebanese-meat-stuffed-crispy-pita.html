Arayes (Lebanese Meat-Stuffed Crispy Pita)

Why This Recipe

Lebanese street food that's like a spiced quesadilla — raw seasoned kofta stuffed inside pita pockets, then pan-fried until the outside is golden and crispy while the meat cooks through. Bold Middle Eastern spices, quick 30-minute cook time, and a whipped tahini-yogurt dipping sauce that's pure magic.

Ingredients

  • 5 thin pocket pita breads (~15cm/6" diameter), cut in half
  • Olive oil spray (or olive oil for brushing)
  • 1/2 brown onion, grated
  • 500g (1 lb) lamb or beef mince (ground meat)
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley
  • 3 tsp ground coriander
  • 3 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika (or plain paprika)
  • 3/4 tsp ground allspice (or mixed spice)
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (reduce to 1/4 tsp for milder)
  • 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 cup plain yogurt (for sauce)
  • 2 tbsp tahini (for sauce)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (for sauce)
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated (for sauce)
  • 1/2 tsp salt (for sauce)

Instructions

Prep the Filling

  • Grate the brown onion using a box grater into a large bowl — you want the juices as well as the onion flesh (not chopped, grated).
  • Add the lamb or beef mince, grated garlic, parsley, ground coriander, ground cumin, smoked paprika, ground allspice, cayenne pepper, and salt.
  • Mix everything thoroughly with your hands for 1-2 minutes until the meat is evenly seasoned and combined.

Stuff the Pitas

  • Divide the meat mixture into 10 equal portions (about 55-60g / 1/4 cup each).
  • Flatten each portion into a thin semi-circle shape slightly smaller than a pita half.
  • Gently open each pita pocket — if resistant, microwave for 15 seconds to soften, then run a butter knife inside the slit to pry it open.
  • Place a flattened meat portion inside each pita pocket. Close and press gently to spread the meat evenly to the edges, keeping it thin and flat.

Pan-Fry

  • Preheat oven to 50°C / 120°F. Place a wire rack on a baking tray (to keep cooked arayes warm without getting soggy).
  • Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
  • Spray both sides of the stuffed pitas with olive oil (or brush with oil).
  • Place 2-3 pitas in the pan (fit as many as possible in a single layer).
  • Cook for about 2 minutes on each side, pressing down lightly with a spatula, until golden and crispy. The thin meat layer cooks through in this time.
  • Transfer cooked arayes to the wire rack in the warm oven. Repeat with remaining pitas.

Make the Whipped Tahini Sauce

  • In a heatproof bowl, whisk together yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, grated garlic, and salt until smooth.
  • Microwave for 15 seconds on high to warm slightly.
  • Whisk again vigorously — the sauce will become light and airy, almost like soft whipped cream.

Serve

  • Pile the arayes onto a serving platter.
  • Serve whole or cut in half, alongside the whipped tahini-yogurt dipping sauce.
  • Optional: pair with a simple Greek salad or fresh tomato and cucumber salad.

Pro Tips

  • Grate the onion instead of chopping it — the fine texture means it cooks through with the meat (no pre-cooking needed) and the extra onion juices make the filling more flavorful.
  • The meat is spread so thin it cooks in just 4 minutes total. If your pitas are thick, make sure to press the filling flat — if you're worried, cook 30 seconds longer per side.
  • Freeze pre-stuffed raw pitas on a tray, then transfer to a bag. Cook straight from frozen in the pan or air fryer at 190°C for 8-10 minutes — perfect for meal prep.
