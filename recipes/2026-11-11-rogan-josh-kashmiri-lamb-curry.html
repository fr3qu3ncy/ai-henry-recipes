Rogan Josh (Kashmiri Lamb Curry)

Why This Recipe

Winter's definitive lamb dish from the Kashmiri wazwan feast — deep red from Kashmiri chilli, fragrant with whole cinnamon, cardamom and cloves. Slow-cooked and rich, a proper cold-weather curry that's different from anything on the site yet.

Ingredients

  • 3½ tbsp ghee (or butter)
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 6 green cardamom pods, lightly bruised
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, finely grated
  • 5 tbsp tomato passata
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 750g boneless lamb shoulder, cut into 3cm cubes
  • 350ml (1½ cups) low-salt chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • ¾ tsp chilli powder (or to taste)
  • 4 tsp ground coriander
  • 4 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp turmeric powder
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp ground fennel
  • 125ml (½ cup) plain yoghurt
  • ½ tsp extra garam masala (for finishing)
  • ½ tsp extra ground fennel (for finishing)
  • Chopped coriander, to serve
  • Basmati rice, to serve

Instructions

Prep

  • Cut the lamb shoulder into even 3cm cubes — even pieces cook evenly.
  • Bruise the cardamom pods, finely mince the garlic, finely grate the ginger and finely chop the onion.

Build the base

  • Melt the ghee in a large heavy-based pot over medium heat. Add the cinnamon stick, cardamom pods and cloves and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Add the onion and cook for 7 minutes, stirring frequently, until golden and browning at the edges.
  • Add the garlic and grated ginger and cook for a further 1 minute until fragrant.

Spice & simmer

  • Stir in the paprika, chilli powder, ground coriander, cumin, turmeric, nutmeg, garam masala and fennel. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly so the spices toast and don't burn.
  • Mix in the tomato passata and salt, then pour in the stock and stir to combine.
  • Add the lamb cubes, stir, and bring to a gentle simmer.
  • Put the lid on and reduce the heat to low–medium-low so it simmers gently. Cook for 1 hour 45 minutes, giving an occasional stir, until the lamb is very tender — it should pry apart easily with two forks.

Finish & serve

  • Remove the lid and cook for a further 15 minutes to reduce and thicken the sauce slightly.
  • Stir in the yoghurt, extra garam masala and extra fennel. Cook for a few more minutes until the sauce is glossy.
  • Discard the cinnamon stick and cardamom pods. Ladle over steamed basmati rice, sprinkle with fresh coriander and serve.

Pro Tips

  • Ghee (clarified butter) is the secret weapon here — it carries the spice aromas far better than plain oil and adds a nutty layer to the sauce. Worth picking up a jar; you'll end up using it for everything.
  • Don't skip toasting the ground spices for those 30 seconds — that's where the deep, dark red colour and flavour come from. Watch it closely: it goes from toasted to burnt in seconds.
