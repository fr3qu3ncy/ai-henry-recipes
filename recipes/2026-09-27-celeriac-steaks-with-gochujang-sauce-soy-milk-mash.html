Celeriac Steaks with Gochujang Sauce & Soy-Milk Mash

Why This Recipe

Celeriac is at its peak right now in late September, and this is a great way to stop treating it like a mash vegetable — thick steaks scored diamond-style, marinated in smoked red wine, and finished with a glossy gochujang jus. A Korean-umami take on a British seasonal staple that cooks mostly hands-off in the oven.

Ingredients

  • 800g celeriac, root end trimmed
  • 120ml dry red wine (e.g. Merlot or Rioja)
  • 80ml olive oil
  • 1 tbsp liquid smoke (or 1 tsp smoked paprika)
  • 60ml soy sauce
  • 1½ tbsp maple syrup
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tbsp coarsely cracked black peppercorns
  • 750g Maris Piper potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 55g butter (or vegan butter)
  • 50ml soy milk (for the mash)
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 80ml soy milk (for the sauce)

Instructions

Prep

  • Wash and dry the celeriac well, then slice into four ½-inch-thick discs. Carefully slice off the outside skin, removing no more than ½cm from each disc.
  • Score shallow lines ½cm apart across the top of each disc with a sharp knife, then rotate each disc 45° and repeat so the scores form rough diamonds. Set aside.
  • Whisk together the wine, olive oil, liquid smoke, soy sauce, maple syrup, thyme and cracked peppercorns to make the marinade. Transfer three-quarters to a bowl and set aside.
  • Put the celeriac discs in a large ziplock bag, pour in the remaining quarter of the marinade (adding a little more from the bowl if not fully coated), seal and refrigerate for 1-4 hours.

Cook

  • Heat the oven to 200°C / fan 180°C / gas 6 and line a baking tray with baking paper.
  • Remove the celeriac from the marinade and place on the tray, scored-side up. Brush with a little extra marinade and roast for 15 minutes.
  • Turn the discs over, brush with more marinade and roast for a further 15 minutes. Reserve the remaining marinade for the sauce.
  • While the celeriac roasts, cook the potatoes in a large pan of boiling water for 20 minutes until a knife slides easily into the centres. Drain and leave to steam in the colander for 5 minutes.
  • Return the potatoes to the pan and mash well until smooth. Stir in the butter, then the 50ml of soy milk a splash at a time (you may not need it all). Season with salt and pepper and cover to keep warm.

Finish

  • To make the sauce, whisk the gochujang and the 80ml of soy milk into the reserved marinade.
  • Warm in a medium, high-sided frying pan over medium heat, bring to a simmer and reduce by half, stirring often but without boiling, until glossy and chocolatey-brown. If it thickens too much, loosen with a little extra wine.

Serve

  • Spoon the mash onto plates, lay the celeriac steaks scored-side up on top and spoon over a generous serving of the gochujang sauce.

Pro Tips

  • The diamond scoring isn't decorative — it creates more surface area for the marinade and the glaze to catch, and it helps the thick steaks roast through evenly in just 30 minutes.
  • If you don't have liquid smoke, a good-quality smoked paprika in the marinade gets you 80% of the effect — but don't add both, or it turns bitter.
