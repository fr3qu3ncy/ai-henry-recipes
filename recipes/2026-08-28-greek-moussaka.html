Greek Moussaka

Why This Recipe

Late August is peak season for aubergine and tomatoes, and classic Greek moussaka is the definitive aubergine showpiece — lamb, cinnamon and oregano layered with golden-fried aubergine, par-boiled potato and a nutmeg parmesan béchamel, then baked until deep golden. Big hands-off oven time, minimal active effort, and it serves a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 6 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for drizzling
  • 3 medium aubergines, cut into 5mm rounds
  • 800g lamb mince
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 fat garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 heaped tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 200ml red wine
  • 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1/2 tbsp light brown soft sugar
  • 550g Maris Piper potatoes, peeled and sliced into 5mm rounds
  • 40g unsalted butter
  • 40g plain flour
  • 450ml whole milk
  • 40g parmesan, finely grated
  • whole nutmeg, for grating
  • 1 large egg plus 1 yolk, lightly beaten

Instructions

Cook the Aubergine

  • Heat a frying pan over high heat. Drizzle 4 tbsp of the olive oil over the aubergine slices and fry them in several batches for 5-7 mins, or until golden brown and beginning to soften - do not crowd the pan, or they will steam instead of crisp. Add a dash more oil if they look dry. Set aside on a plate lined with kitchen paper.

Cook the Lamb Sauce

  • Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large flameproof casserole over medium-high heat. Add the lamb mince and fry for 8-10 mins until a deep golden brown, stirring and breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Tip into a bowl and set aside.
  • Add the remaining oil to the casserole. Tip in the onion with a pinch of salt and fry gently for 10-12 mins until softened and translucent. Add the garlic, oregano, cinnamon and bay leaves, cooking for a further 1 min.
  • Return the lamb to the pan and pour in the red wine. Bring to a bubble and let it reduce by half, then stir through the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée, brown sugar and 200ml water. Season, lower the heat and simmer gently, uncovered, for 20 mins, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened.

Prep the Potato and Béchamel

  • Heat the oven to 200C/180 fan/gas 4. Bring a large pan of lightly salted water to the boil, add the potato slices and cook for 6 mins. Drain and leave to steam-dry for 10 mins.
  • Melt the butter in a small saucepan, stir in the flour and cook over medium heat for 1 min. Whisk in the milk a little at a time until smooth, then bring to a simmer and cook for 3 mins. Remove from the heat and whisk through the parmesan, a little grated nutmeg, seasoning, and finally the egg and yolk.

Assemble and Bake

  • Take a large rectangular ovenproof dish. Spoon a third of the lamb sauce in and spread out evenly, then layer half the aubergine and half the potato. Add the remaining lamb sauce, another layer of aubergine, then the potato. Spoon the béchamel over the top and smooth with a palette knife.
  • Bake in the centre of the oven for 50 mins, until deep golden brown - cover with foil if it browns too much. Set aside for 10 mins to cool before serving.

Pro Tips

  • Fry the aubergine in small batches, never crowded - overcrowding steams the slices and kills the golden crust that stops the layers from going watery.
  • The 10-minute rest before slicing is non-negotiable: it lets the layers set so you get clean, tidy slices instead of a jumbled mess on the plate.
