Deep Midwinter Stilton Pie

Why This Recipe

Leeks at their sweetest and Stilton in full midwinter glory make this the definitive deep-autumn pie — a layered mash-and-blue-cheese build under crisp puff pastry that is far easier than it looks.

Ingredients

  • 600g maincrop potatoes
  • 75g unsalted butter, plus extra for greasing
  • 500g leeks, trimmed weight
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves
  • 500g bought puff pastry
  • 200g Stilton cheese, crumbled
  • 1 egg yolk, blended with 1 tbsp water
  • Sea salt and black pepper

Instructions

Prep

  • Peel the potatoes, cut into even chunks and cook in a large pan of boiling salted water until tender (about 15 min, knife slides through easily).
  • Drain in a colander, leave a few minutes to steam-dry, then coarsely mash. Stir in a third of the butter and some seasoning; transfer to a bowl to cool.
  • Thinly slice the leeks (white and green parts) and finely chop the garlic.
  • Melt the remaining butter in a large frying pan over medium-low heat. Add the leeks, garlic and thyme, season, and fry for about 10 minutes until softened but not browned. Transfer to a bowl to cool.
  • Preheat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Have a 20cm loose-bottom cake tin (at least 6cm deep) at hand.

Assemble

  • Set aside a third of the pastry. Roll the remainder on a floured surface into a circle big enough to line the tin with about 5cm overhang; lay it in the tin.
  • Spoon in the mashed potato and gently press down without over-compressing.
  • Crumble the Stilton over the potato, then spoon in the cooled leeks on top, spreading evenly.
  • Roll out the remaining pastry and, using the tin as a guide, cut out a pie lid to sit on top of the leeks. Tuck it in place and brush with egg mixture.
  • Trim the pastry sides to leave a 3cm rim all round, then fold the rim down over the lid to seal. Brush the rim with egg; decorate with stars or leaves cut from the trimmings if you like. Cut a few small slits in the top for steam.

Bake & Serve

  • Bake for 40-45 minutes until puffed and deeply golden all over.
  • Rest for 5 minutes, then serve hot or warm straight from the tin with a dressed green salad or roast vegetables.

Pro Tips

  • Build the pie up to a day ahead, cover and chill, then add a few minutes to the bake time — the pastry sets and is even easier to handle cold.
  • Let both the mash and leek mix cool fully before assembling: a hot filling melts the pastry base and gives you a soggy pie instead of a crisp one.
