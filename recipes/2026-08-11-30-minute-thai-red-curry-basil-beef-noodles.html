Why This Recipe

A fiery, aromatic Thai street-food classic reimagined as a quick weeknight bowl — ground beef stir-fried with red curry paste, served over rice noodles tossed in spicy herb oil, topped with pickled carrot-tomato salad and cashews. Different from green curry, this channels the bold, punchy flavors of Thai pad kra pao with noodles. Ready in 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 6 oz rice noodles
  • 1/2 cup sesame or avocado oil
  • 1/3 cup green onions, sliced (white and green parts separated)
  • 1-3 tbsp Thai red curry paste (adjust to heat preference)
  • 4 cloves garlic, grated
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1/2 cup fresh Thai basil or regular basil, chopped
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • Pinch of chili flakes
  • 4 strips bacon, chopped
  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 fat ratio)
  • 1/3 cup tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 cups shredded carrots
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 cup mixed Thai basil and cilantro leaves
  • 1/2 cup roasted salted cashews, chopped

Instructions

Prep the Pickled Salad

  • In a medium bowl, combine shredded carrots, halved cherry tomatoes, toasted sesame oil, lime juice, rice vinegar, and mixed Thai basil and cilantro leaves.
  • Toss well to coat everything in the dressing. Set aside — the acid will gently pickle the vegetables while you cook.

Make the Curry Herb Oil

  • In a large skillet, heat 1/2 cup sesame or avocado oil over medium heat until hot but not smoking.
  • While the oil heats, combine sliced green onion whites, Thai red curry paste, grated garlic, grated ginger, chopped basil, cilantro, and a pinch of chili flakes in a large heatproof bowl.
  • Carefully pour the hot oil over the herb mixture — it will sizzle and release incredible aromas. Stir to combine and set aside.

Cook the Beef

  • In the same skillet (don't wipe it clean), cook the chopped bacon over medium heat until crisp, about 4-5 minutes.
  • Add the ground beef to the skillet with the bacon. Cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned and cooked through, about 5 minutes.
  • Remove from heat and stir in the tamari or soy sauce. Set aside.

Cook the Noodles

  • Cook rice noodles according to package directions (usually 3-4 minutes in boiling water). Drain well.
  • Immediately toss the hot noodles with the curry herb oil mixture, ensuring every strand is coated.

Assemble & Serve

  • Divide the curry-coated noodles among 4 bowls.
  • Top each bowl generously with the beef and bacon mixture.
  • Spoon the pickled carrot-tomato salad over the top.
  • Sprinkle with chopped roasted cashews and any remaining green onion greens.
  • Serve immediately while hot.

Pro Tips

  • The curry herb oil is the secret weapon — pouring hot oil over fresh herbs, garlic, and curry paste creates an explosive flavor that coats the noodles. Don't skip this step.
  • Adjust the Thai red curry paste to your heat preference: 1 tbsp for mild, 2 tbsp for medium, 3 tbsp for spicy. Martin's profile suggests 2 tbsp for aromatic dishes.
