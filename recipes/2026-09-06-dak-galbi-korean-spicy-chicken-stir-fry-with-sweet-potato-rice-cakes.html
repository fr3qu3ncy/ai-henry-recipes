Dak Galbi (Korean Spicy Chicken Stir-Fry with Sweet Potato & Rice Cakes)

Why This Recipe

The ultimate Korean street-food party dish — spicy gochujang-marinated chicken stir-fried with sweet potato, carrot and chewy rice cakes, straight out of Chuncheon's famous galbi pits. A proper hands-on wok job that's perfect for a Friday night with cold soju, and it slots right into your current Korean phase.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken thigh fillets, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1/2 sweet potato (180g), cut into long thick sticks (like English chips)
  • 1/2 carrot (60g), diagonally sliced
  • 1/4 cabbage (320g), shredded
  • 18 pieces Korean rice cakes (175g), separated (soak pre-packaged ones in warm water 10 mins)
  • 10 leaves Korean perilla (35g), thinly sliced (sub: fresh basil)
  • 2-3 tbsp cooking oil (rice bran oil if you have it)
  • 3 tbsp gochujang (Korean chilli paste)
  • 2 tbsp rice wine (mirin or sake)
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp raw sugar
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp minced ginger
  • 1 tsp Korean curry powder (or mild yellow curry powder)
  • 1/2 onion (35g), grated or minced
  • Black pepper
  • Steamed rice to serve, plus lettuce, sliced garlic and ssamjang for wraps (optional)

Instructions

Prep

  • Cut the chicken thighs into bite-size pieces, sweet potato into thick sticks, slice the carrot on the diagonal, and shred the cabbage.
  • Whisk together the gochujang, rice wine, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar, garlic, ginger, Korean curry powder, grated onion and a few sprinkles of black pepper into a smooth marinade.
  • Pour the marinade over the chicken, toss to coat, and marinate for at least 30 minutes — 4 hours or overnight is much better if you have the time.
  • If using pre-packaged rice cakes, separate them and soak in warm water for 10 minutes.

Cook

  • Heat a large skillet or wok (ideally a 12-inch cast iron pan) over medium-high heat and add the cooking oil until shimmering.
  • Add all the vegetables and rice cakes, then pile the marinated chicken on top. Cook 3-4 minutes undisturbed, stirring a few times.
  • Reduce the heat to medium-low and cook a further 10-15 minutes, stirring often so nothing sticks. Cabbage and rice cakes will be done first, sweet potato last — don't wait for everything to be perfect or the first to finish will turn mushy.
  • Cover with a lid for the last few minutes to speed things up if the pan is running dry — the sweet potato should be tender and lightly caramelised at the edges.

Serve

  • Tip the whole pan onto the table (restaurant style) or divide between bowls with steamed rice on the side.
  • Tear in the sliced perilla leaves for the fresh, anise-like finish.
  • For the full experience, wrap spoonfuls in lettuce with sliced raw garlic and ssamjang, Korean BBQ style.

Pro Tips

  • The long marinade is the whole game — 4+ hours or overnight takes the chicken from good to properly deep-spiced. Short on time? 30 minutes still works, but don't skip it.
  • Serve it at the table in a cast iron pan over a burner so it keeps bubbling as you eat — the sweet potato keeps cooking and caramelising, and the rice cakes turn extra chewy at the edges. Add a splash of stock or water if it starts to stick.
