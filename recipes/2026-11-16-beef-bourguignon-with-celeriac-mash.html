Beef Bourguignon with Celeriac Mash

Why This Recipe

A hands-off winter classic that fits your love of long, low-effort oven braises (moussaka, bobotie). All-wine, no-stock gravy is the trick, and the celeriac mash swaps mash potato for something more interesting.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp goose fat
  • 600g shin beef, cut into large chunks
  • 100g smoked streaky bacon, sliced
  • 350g shallots or pearl onions, peeled
  • 250g chestnut mushrooms (about 20)
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 bouquet garni (thyme, parsley, bay leaves, tied together)
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 750ml red wine (Burgundy is good)
  • 600g celeriac
  • 2 tbsp olive oil plus a glug
  • 1-2 sprigs rosemary and thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cardamom pods

Instructions

Prep

  • Tie a bouquet garni from a few thyme sprigs, parsley and 2 bay leaves.
  • Season the shin beef chunks with salt and pepper.
  • Peel the shallots/pearl onions and slice the garlic; halve the chestnut mushrooms.

Braise

  • Heat a large casserole pan and add the goose fat.
  • Fry the beef in 2-3 batches until golden brown all over (3-5 mins per side), adding a little more fat if needed; transfer to a colander set over a bowl as you go.
  • In the same pan, fry the bacon, shallots, mushrooms, sliced garlic and bouquet garni until lightly browned.
  • Stir in the tomato purée and cook for a few minutes to enrich the base.
  • Return the beef and any drained juices to the pan and stir through.
  • Pour over the 750ml red wine plus about 100ml water so the meat bobs up from the liquid but isn't fully covered; bring to the boil and scrape the caramelised juices from the pan bottom.

Cook

  • Heat the oven to 150C / 130C fan / gas 2.
  • Make a cartouche: lay a square of foil over the casserole to cover the stew and trim the excess.
  • Bake for 3 hours until the beef is tender and falling apart.

Celeriac Mash

  • Peel the celeriac and cut into cubes.
  • Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large frying pan and fry the celeriac for 5 mins until golden; season well with salt and pepper.
  • Stir in the rosemary, thyme, bay leaves and cardamom pods, then pour over 200ml water to nearly cover.
  • Turn the heat to low, partially cover and simmer 25-30 mins until soft and most of the water has evaporated.
  • Drain any remaining water, remove the herb sprigs, bay and cardamom, then lightly crush with a potato masher and finish with a glug of olive oil.

Finish & Serve

  • If the stew sauce looks watery, remove the beef and veg with a slotted spoon, boil the sauce on high for a few mins to thicken, then return the meat.
  • Spoon the beef bourguignon into serving bowls with a large spoonful of celeriac mash on top.

Pro Tips

  • All wine, no stock — it's the key to a deep, rich gravy. If the bottle runs out, top up with a little water rather than stock.
  • Use a proper bouquet garni (tied herb bundle) and pull it out at the end; it infuses flavour without leaving bits in the stew. The celeriac mash is far more interesting than plain mash — don't skip the cardamom.
