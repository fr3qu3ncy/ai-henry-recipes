Spicy Queso Black Bean Sweet Potato Chili

Why This Recipe

Peak sweet potato season meets a creamy queso twist on classic black bean chili — smoky, a little spicy, and loaded with texture. A 40-minute weeknight pot that tastes like it simmered all day.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons salted butter
  • 2 yellow onions, diced
  • 3-4 sweet potatoes, cubed (3-4 cups)
  • 3 tablespoons chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 2 cups red salsa
  • 1 can (8 oz) tomato paste
  • 2 cans (4 oz) diced green chilies
  • 2-3 cups broth (chicken or vegetable)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt
  • 2 cans black beans, undrained
  • 6 oz cream cheese, room temperature
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • To serve: avocado, Greek yogurt, cilantro, green onions

Instructions

Prep

  • Dice the onions and cube the sweet potatoes to about 1/2-inch pieces.
  • Soften the cream cheese (dice it if needed) so it folds in smoothly, and shred the cheddar.

Cook

  • In a large pot over medium-high heat, cook the butter, onions, sweet potatoes, chili powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin and cayenne together, about 5 minutes, until the onions begin to soften.
  • Stir in the salsa, tomato paste and green chilies. Pour in 2 cups of broth, add the bay leaves and season with salt.
  • Bring to a simmer, reduce heat to low, partially cover and cook 20-30 minutes, until the sweet potatoes are tender. Longer low-and-slow simmering deepens the flavour — add more broth as it thickens.

Finish

  • Remove the bay leaves, then stir in the undrained black beans, cream cheese and cheddar. Cook about 5 minutes, stirring, until the cheeses are fully melted and the chili turns creamy and glossy.

Serve

  • Ladle into bowls and top with avocado, a dollop of Greek yogurt, cilantro and green onions. Serve warm.

Pro Tips

  • The undrained black beans are the secret to body and creaminess — never drain them. The added liquid is exactly what the stew wants.
  • Let it simmer 40-60 minutes instead of 20-30 if you have the time: the spices mellow, the sweet potatoes soften into the sauce, and the whole thing tastes twice as good. It also improves massively in the fridge next day.
