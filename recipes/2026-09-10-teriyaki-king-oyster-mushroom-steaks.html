Teriyaki King Oyster Mushroom Steaks

Why This Recipe

King oyster mushroom 'steaks' are the umami upgrade on a quick pan dish — scored and seared like meat, then glazed in a sticky teriyaki sauce. 25 minutes door-to-plate, perfect weeknight technique play.

Ingredients

  • 4 large king oyster mushrooms (cleaned, trimmed, sliced in half lengthwise)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (or sesame oil for extra flavour)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup (or honey if not vegan)
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • Sesame seeds and thinly sliced spring onions, to serve
  • Steamed rice or noodles, to serve (optional)

Instructions

Prep

  • Slice each king oyster mushroom in half lengthwise.
  • Lightly score the flat cut sides in a crisscross pattern — this helps them soak up the sauce and caramelise beautifully.
  • In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, maple syrup, rice vinegar, brown sugar, cornstarch, water, garlic and ginger until smooth. Set aside.

Cook

  • Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  • Place the mushrooms cut-side down and cook for 3-4 minutes without moving them, until golden and slightly crisp.
  • Flip and cook for another 2-3 minutes on the other side.

Finish

  • Pour the sauce into the pan, reduce the heat to medium, and stir the mushrooms frequently as the sauce thickens and turns glossy — about 2-3 minutes until it clings to the mushrooms.
  • Serve hot over steamed rice or noodles, sprinkled with sesame seeds and spring onions.

Pro Tips

  • The crisscross scoring is the whole trick — it creates ridges that grab the glaze and caramelise the edges, giving that 'steak' look and texture.
  • Swap olive oil for sesame oil and it becomes a proper Japanese-style glaze; a splash of mirin in place of the maple syrup takes it further.
